Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 8 November 2026

The Meta app “ProblemAgent Yayın” (operated by HappySproutStore LLC) only stores comments or messages that people send to the owner's own Facebook Pages or Instagram accounts, together with the sender's public username and ID, so they can be answered. No other Facebook or Instagram user data is collected.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to happysproutstore@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request”.
  2. Include your Facebook or Instagram username so the related records can be found.
  3. All stored comments, messages and identifiers linked to you will be deleted within 30 days, and you will receive a confirmation email.

You can also remove the app's access at any time from your Facebook settings: Settings & privacy › Settings › Apps and websites.

See also the privacy policy.
