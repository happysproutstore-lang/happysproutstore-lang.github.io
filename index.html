ProblemAgent

A private content-operations tool of HappySproutStore LLC

ProblemAgent is an internal tool used by its owner to manage content for a small number of YouTube channels that the owner operates. It is not offered to the public and has no user sign-ups.

What it does with Google / YouTube

  • Uploads videos that the owner has produced to the owner's own YouTube channels.
  • Reads basic channel information to confirm that each upload goes to the correct channel.
  • Reads view and engagement statistics (YouTube Analytics) for those channels so the owner can see how their content performs.

What it does with Amazon (Echo Sprout Store)

HappySproutStore LLC sells original print-on-demand t-shirt designs on Amazon (Echo Sprout Store, US marketplace). ProblemAgent uses the Amazon Selling Partner API only for this one seller account:

  • Creates and updates our own product listings, only after the owner has reviewed and approved each design and listing text.
  • Reads our own orders, inventory, account performance and financial reports to produce an internal daily summary.

It does not request or store Amazon buyers' personal information and does not share Amazon data with any third party.

See the Privacy Policy for how data is handled.
